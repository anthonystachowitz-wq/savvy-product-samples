About this sample

A working product sample for one RIA transition workflow. It is not a Savvy product and it does not touch real client data.

Unofficial concept November 2026

Transition Copilot is a concept for the operations layer an RIA needs when an adviser joins with a book built somewhere else. The sample starts with six synthetic documents and a CRM export. It ends with a plan, a review queue, and an evidence trail.

This page lists what runs, what is simulated, where the guardrails are, and what is missing. The sample is built so those claims can be checked against the data and the code.

Who built this

Anthony Stachowitz

Product management work sample · November 2026

I built this to show how I think about transition operations. The record comes first. The plan follows from the record. The approval trail is part of the workflow, not a report added at the end.

I did not work at Savvy and did not use any non-public Savvy information. The data is generated, the custodians are fictional, and the policy references are synthetic. The brief covers the market context, the competition, and the decisions I would defend in a review.
