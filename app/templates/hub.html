How to look at this in five minutes

  1. Start with Transition Copilot. It is the finished one: a signed book becomes a record, a signature plan, and an approval file.
  2. Then Client Concierge. Same client record, now answering a client question with citations and an advice boundary.
  3. Book IQ ranks the next dollar in every book. Supervision puts generated marketing through a pre-approval file. LP Ops takes private markets paperwork from intake to capital call.
  4. Read How it is built to see the model, the prompts, the evals, and what the models got wrong.

Why these five

They are the five levers in Savvy's own operating model: recruit advisers, grow their books, serve their clients, keep marketing compliant, and scale operations without adding headcount per dollar.

Every surface reads the same synthetic book and writes to the same audit trail. That is the product argument: one client record, many surfaces, one set of controls.

All metrics on this site are modelled estimates from stated assumptions. Nothing here is a measurement.

What is real and what is simulated

Real

  • The application code, the model calls, the prompts, the evals, and the approval and audit logic.
  • Every model output was produced by a live call to DeepSeek and is replayed from the recording by default.
  • The synthetic book is generated deterministically and is internally consistent.

Simulated

  • All clients, households, accounts, custodians, LPs, and adviser data are invented.
  • There is no custodian connection, no filing, no trading, and no delivery of any message.
  • The business metrics are modelled estimates, not measurements from a live firm.
