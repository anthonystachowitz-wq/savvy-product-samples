The thesis

An adviser joins with a book built somewhere else. The transfer itself is not the hard part. Account opening, form packets, signatures, consent, and reconciliation are.

This sample shows one book moving through that work. Six documents and a CRM export become a household record with confidence and flags. The record becomes a plan and a risk list. Every outgoing draft waits for a named approver and an evidence file.

It is a product sample, not a live service. There is no custodian connection and no client data. This module runs deterministic extraction and templates; the other four surfaces show the recorded live model calls.
