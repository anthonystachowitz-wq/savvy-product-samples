What the model does not claim

  • No time study. The minutes are an input, not a measurement.
  • No public NIGO rate exists. The plan instruments risk from day one instead of inventing one.
  • No public days-to-first-funded series exists. The 45-day target is a seeded planning window.
  • Vendor retention data is account-level, not asset-level. This page does not use it as a claim.
